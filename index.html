02 / My story

Every journey has
a beginning.

A growing story about education, inspiration and the decision to spend more time building digital projects.

2025 – 2026

SMK Alang Iskandar

During this period, I studied at SMK Alang Iskandar. My school journey formed part of my wider experience as I continued discovering my interests and developing myself.

A TURNING POINT

A teacher's recommendation

My English teacher suggested that I participate in the Coolest Projects competition. I did not manage to enter the competition, but the recommendation encouraged me to focus more seriously on developing my own technology projects.

2026 ONWARDS

Learning through projects

I continued exploring programming and digital creation. Working on ideas such as Smart Academy and Smart Car Hub became part of my learning journey. I continue to develop my skills and refine my approach as I learn.

LATE 2026

SMK Abu Bakar Al-Baqir

Towards the end of 2026, I continued my education at SMK Abu Bakar Al-Baqir. This marked another chapter in my school journey, with new experiences and opportunities to keep growing.

"I may have missed the competition, but I didn't give up on the idea of building something of my own."
