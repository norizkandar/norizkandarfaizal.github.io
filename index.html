01 / About me

A curious mind with an interest in building digital things.

I am still learning, experimenting and discovering where technology can take me.

My name is Mohamad Norizkandar bin Mohamad Faizal. I use the name Mohamad Norizkandar across my personal digital presence.

I am interested in application development, websites and the process of transforming an idea into something people can explore and use. I enjoy learning how digital products are structured, how interfaces work and how different technologies connect.

My learning journey includes experimenting with JavaScript, React Native, Expo and Supabase, alongside web technologies such as HTML and CSS. My projects are opportunities to practise these skills and understand what it takes to develop a more complete product.

I do not see a portfolio as a claim that I already know everything. I see it as a record of what I have explored, what I am building and how I am progressing.

My approach

Stay curious. Learn the fundamentals. Build real projects. Be honest about what works and keep improving what does not.

02 / My story

From curiosity to creating digital experiences.

Every developer starts somewhere. My story is not about having everything figured out — it is about discovering technology, learning through experimentation and finding the motivation to keep creating.

01

The beginning: curiosity

My journey began with learning and exploring the digital world around me. Over time, I became interested in more than simply using websites and applications. I wanted to understand how they were made and how I could learn to create my own.

02

A suggestion that made me think bigger

During my time at school, my English teacher suggested that I consider joining Coolest Projects. I did not end up participating, but the suggestion stayed with me as an idea worth thinking about: technology could be a way to turn personal ideas into something tangible.

That moment became part of the inspiration behind my interest in making projects and exploring software development further.

03

Learning through building

Rather than waiting until I knew everything, I began exploring technologies and building projects as a way to learn. I experimented with websites, application concepts, user interfaces and the tools used to develop digital products.

Projects such as Eonix Tech Studio, Smart Academy and Smart Car Hub became part of that process. Each project brought different questions, challenges and opportunities to practise.

04

Understanding that development takes time

Building software has taught me that an idea and a finished product are not the same thing. A design can look promising while features still need work. A prototype can help explain an idea without being production-ready.

I want my portfolio to reflect that honestly. Some projects are experiments or works in progress, and there is still plenty for me to learn.

05

Building the next chapter

Today, I continue developing my skills, documenting my work and improving my online portfolio. My goal is to build a stronger foundation in software development, create more useful digital experiences and keep learning from every project I take on.

“I am not trying to pretend that I know everything. I am building, learning and becoming better with every step.”

— Mohamad Norizkandar
