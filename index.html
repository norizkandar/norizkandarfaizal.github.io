Hello, I'm Mohamad Norizkandar.

I'm an aspiring digital builder with a strong interest in software, mobile applications, web experiences and emerging technology. I enjoy exploring how ideas can become useful digital products.

My journey is built around learning by doing: exploring development tools, experimenting with interfaces, building prototypes and improving projects step by step. Each project gives me a new opportunity to understand both the technical and creative sides of building something.

Through my personal projects and Eonix Tech Studio, I am developing a portfolio of experiments and digital experiences. I believe progress comes from staying curious, being willing to learn and continuing to improve the work.
