Curiosity is where everything starts.

My name is Mohamad Norizkandar. I am a student with an interest in programming, application development and creative technology. I enjoy exploring how digital products work and discovering how an idea can become an interactive experience.

My learning journey includes experimenting with JavaScript, React Native, Expo, Supabase and web technologies. Along the way, I have worked on concepts for educational applications, automotive platforms and my own digital brand.

I do not see every project as a finished product. Some are prototypes, some are still being developed and others are ideas I want to explore further. Each one gives me a chance to learn, solve problems and improve my technical skills.

My goal is to continue growing as a developer, create useful digital experiences and build a portfolio that reflects genuine effort, curiosity and progress.

04 / MY JOURNEY

From curiosity to creation.

Learning programming is an ongoing process of exploration, experimentation and problem-solving.

Learning by building real projects.

My coding journey grew from an interest in technology and the desire to create digital products of my own. Instead of only learning concepts, I began experimenting with websites, mobile application frameworks and online development tools.

As projects became more complex, I encountered practical challenges such as application structure, authentication, databases, user interfaces, deployment and debugging. Working through these challenges has become an important part of my learning experience.

I am still learning. I use my projects to practise new concepts, test ideas and identify areas that need improvement. My aim is to keep developing stronger technical foundations over time.

"Build what you can today. Learn what you need for tomorrow. Improve what you made yesterday."
